WELCOME TO MY SCHOOL DIARY

Education is a new step of reform, one step towards change



FEATURES:

    1. Time Table

  • Teacher can directly upload Time Table through his/her admin panel.
  • No Headache to mention each and every lectures and subjects.
  • All the students of his/her class will be able to see that time table.



    2. Homework

  • Teacher can assign homework instantly.
  • Homework will be directly assigned through panels.
  • Teacher can also assign assignments, practicals, and project work directly through panels to all the students of that class at the same time.



    3. Message

  • School and teacher both can easily send the message to the Student's Parents.
  • School can also send the message to the particular teacher.
  • Updates, Permission slips, Events or any other information can be send through message to their entire class or selected student.



    4. Conversation With Parents

  • Teacher can easily inform the parents by only one click about the Exams , PTMs , Holidays , Events , Complaints and any other useful information.
  • Forget the paper notes. Now teacher can easily contact with Student's parents.



    5. Complaints

  • School or Teacher both can easily tell student complaints directly to their Parents.
  • Easy for Parents to see Reports about his/her Child.



    6. PTM

  • The teacher can make update for the ptm directly through app.
  • Parents can also send response of that PTM either they are coming or not.
  • Teacher can also check about their past PTM records.
  • Teacher can also check the list of parents who attend PTM with dates.
