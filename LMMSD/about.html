WELCOME TO MY SCHOOL DIARY

Let children be creative and make a difference

Welcome to “My School Diary” Application. An effective alternative to mere paper-work at schools. Its main objective is to combine every thick & thin of a communication, including homework and time management for teachers, students and parents at the same time.

It is easily accessible and simple to register tool. You’re only required to compile some details about your education institute. We, as per that information, will list your school in our WProject.

This will allow many user parents to simply search and get their children enroll with your institute. We will compile all the necessary information like school facilities, status of the principal, number of students/teachers, classes, examination procedures to admission formalities.

We have created this instant service so that people can do smart-work, sitting at home by saving their time , energy & money.
